Discord ↔ Telegram Integration Platform

Running in production
PythonDiscord APITelegram Bot APISQLite

Members of a gaming community were split between two messengers and couldn't see each other. The goal was to unify the chats, help players find teammates and handle player requests without an off-the-shelf ticketing system.

  • Two-way routing of messages and events between Discord and Telegram in a single asynchronous service.
  • Player profiles: data collection, profile publishing, teammate matching.
  • Custom ticketing system: requests tied to users, statuses, handling of technical issues.
  • State (profiles, tickets, service data) is stored in SQLite. After a restart the service recovers and keeps running.
  • Result: the community got one shared space to talk and its own support tools, with no third-party services.
